### Food Research Tips
- Research specific restaurants for each food type
- Book omakase in advance (often requires reservation)
- Note English-friendly options
- Consider food tours or guided experiences

### Places & Activities Tips
- Check opening hours and seasonal availability
- Book tickets in advance for popular attractions
- Plan day trips considering travel time
- Have backup indoor activities for bad weather

### Shopping Tips
- Research tax-free shopping options
- Check duty-free allowances
- Consider shipping options for large items
- Note popular souvenirs and local specialties
